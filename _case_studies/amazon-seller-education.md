## The problem

Millions of active merchants rely on Amazon's Seller education to learn how to
succeed in the store. The platform needed to be rebuilt, and finding the right
content was too hard.

## What I did

- **Built the team.** I recruited and led a 40-person organization of
  technical writers and designers, organized under three team leads.
- **Made content findable.** I introduced a structured content taxonomy so
  sellers could find what they needed.
- **Listened and adjusted.** I used NPS feedback to guide ongoing improvements.

## Results

The rebuilt platform reached 95% learner satisfaction, with a feedback loop in
place to keep improving it.
