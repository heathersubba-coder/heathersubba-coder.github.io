## The problem

Liulishuo, an AI-powered English learning app, needed a mobile curriculum that
would work in short sessions on a phone and keep learners engaged over time.

## What I did

- **Designed for mobile.** I built an ESL curriculum from scratch around
  scaffolded, short-form practice lessons.
- **Followed the data.** Based on retention analytics, I pivoted to a
  narrative, travel-focused curriculum to drive engagement.
