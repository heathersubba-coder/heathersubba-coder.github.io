## The problem

The CTO organization's product teams had no dedicated product operations
function. The way teams planned and coordinated work wasn't keeping up with the
pain points engineering was feeling.

## What I did

- **Built the function from scratch.** As the first person in the role, I
  established operating rhythms designed around the organization's real pain
  points rather than a generic playbook.
- **Changed how teams are organized.** I drove leadership to adopt
  value-stream aligned teams.
- **Turned AI strategy into practice.** I ran cross-functional AI build-athons
  that turned AI strategy into core features, and presented the approach to the
  CTO and his leadership team.
- **Raised the team's AI fluency.** I teach product managers an AI-assisted
  roadmap workflow: Claude Code for critique, Gemini for document formatting,
  and Figma Make for interactive prototypes.
- **Led the product team.** As interim head of product, I lead five product
  managers.
