## The problem

AWS wanted cloud skills taught in higher education, but labs and curriculum
only get used if they fit how instructors already teach.

## What I did

- **Started with instructors.** I interviewed university instructors, from
  community colleges to Harvard, about their teaching needs.
- **Designed around their objectives.** I built cloud curriculum and SageMaker
  practice labs that mapped directly to what instructors were trying to teach.

## Results

Aligning the labs with instructors' own learning objectives doubled engagement
year over year.
