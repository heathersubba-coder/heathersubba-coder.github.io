## The problem

Bloomberg users needed one reliable place to learn the product and get answers
without leaving their work. The help they needed existed, but it wasn't easy to
find at the moment they needed it.

## What I did

- **Built it from zero.** I owned the product strategy and roadmap and launched
  the Help Center with a five-person engineering team.
- **Met users where they work.** I built an in-product homepage with
  personalized content recommendations, so help shows up in context instead of
  waiting to be searched for.
- **Let research and data set priorities.** I paired customer interviews with
  usage data to decide what to improve next.
- **Connected it to AI.** I made Help Center content available to Bloomberg's
  in-product AI assistant through MCP (Model Context Protocol), so the assistant
  can answer from the same trusted content.
- **Scaled the content behind it.** Two team leads report to me and run an
  eight-person team of technical writers and content developers producing
  interactive notebooks, API docs, and data science learning tracks.

## Results

The Help Center grew to 8,000 monthly active users. The personalized homepage
now drives 81% of its traffic, and those visitors convert at three times the
rate of people who arrive on their own. Research-driven fixes cut the bounce
rate by 10%.
