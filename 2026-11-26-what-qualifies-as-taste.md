---
title: What Qualifies as Taste?
summary: On Azzedine Alaïa at the Legion of Honor, whether taste can be learned or is something you're born with, and why public art matters in a time of change.
---

Among the more than 80 handmade original designs at *Azzedine Alaïa: Sculpting Fashion* at the Legion of Honor, one central question stood out to me: What qualifies as taste? The sculptural details and craftsmanship are a marvel, but is taste something you can learn, or are you born with it? Can it be cultivated, or do you either have it or you don't? Is taste subjective?

I'm grateful for public exhibitions like this one, which give people up-close access to designs and art they might never otherwise see, and which draw us into public discourse and make us think critically at a time when the human experience is being redefined.
