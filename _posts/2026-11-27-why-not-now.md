---
title: Why Not Now?
summary: On running my first marathon in my late 40s, losing my love for running afterward, and signing up for another.
---

In March 2026, I ran my first full marathon. For years, I thought I would run one, but I never did. Too little time. Too many excuses. Then, suddenly, in my late 40s, I just decided to do it. Why not now?

I didn't have enough time to train, and I missed the marathon I signed up for because of a massive fibroid, but I didn't give up. I ran a local marathon a month after the race I couldn't make. It was painful. It was hard. It sucked. I loved it.

After the marathon, I didn't really want to run. I was worried I'd lost my love for running and that I'd never enter another race. Then, about a month ago, it happened again: marathon fever. I'm setting my sights on an early February 2027 marathon. I still don't have time. I still have a lot of excuses and not enough training ramp. But I'm alive. I'm motivated. Time is precious.
